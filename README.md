# Demon's Plot

- Demon’s Plot is a game dedicated for the horror stories, books and movies
enthusiasts that enjoy a story driven gameplay with an emphasis on survival and
clue finding mechanics. Inspired by some very popular titles from the horror gaming
industry, such as Outlast, Phasmophobia and Slender: The Eight Pages, Demon’s
Plot aims to pack all the essential features from these titles in one single game.

- The story begins with the main character and his girlfriend going to a forest cabin,
located around the Blue Lake. The cabin was his grandfather’s, who died recently
from natural causes. Despite the state of the cabin, it had a deep meaning to him, as
he would have lots of memories from childhood with him and his grandfather. After
enjoying a short break from city life, a scream has broken the peace. Jessi went
missing! The main character must go out to the forest, looking for any clues that may
help find her. During the exploration, he may reveal something sinister.
